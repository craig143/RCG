Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

RCG Advanced Ltd (company number 17040709), registered office London, England, United Kingdom, EC2A 4NE, operates this website at rcgadvanced.com. We are the data controller for any personal data collected through this site. You can contact us at craig@rcgadvanced.com.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, organisation, the nature of your enquiry, and any details you include in your message. We do not collect any other personal data through this site beyond standard technical logs generated automatically by our hosting provider.

Why we collect it

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry and to carry on any resulting business discussion. Our legal basis for processing this data is legitimate interest (responding to enquiries you have chosen to send us) and, where a commercial relationship develops, the steps necessary to enter into a contract.

How we store it

Enquiry form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, and delivered to our email inbox. We do not sell, rent or share your information with any other third party. We retain enquiry data only for as long as necessary to respond to you and maintain a record of the business relationship, after which it is deleted.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run any analytics that profile visitors. Two third-party services are used that may involve limited technical data transfer:

  • Google Fonts: loads a typeface from Google's content delivery network. This may involve your browser contacting Google's servers directly. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree: processes enquiry form submissions on our behalf. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.

Because we do not use non-essential cookies, this site does not display a cookie consent banner.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to ask us what personal data we hold about you, to request correction or deletion of it, and to object to how we use it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at craig@rcgadvanced.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe we have not handled your data properly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

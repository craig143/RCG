Who we are

A global independent intermediary

RCG Advanced is a global independent technology intermediary operating exclusively for security, smart city, public safety and defence organisations. We maintain active commercial relationships with procurement decision-makers, technology providers and government-adjacent organisations worldwide, anchored by deep networks in the United Kingdom, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, the United States, India and South America.

"It is built through trust, sector knowledge and years of relationship development."

Procurement in this sector operates differently. Decisions involve compliance obligations, security classifications and extended approval chains. Buyers do not respond to cold product pitches. Vendors do not gain access through standard distribution. We exist at that intersection, representing both sides through to completion.
